# Changelog

## 1.0.0.0

First public release.

- Hero banner with logos, ratings, running time, genres, muted trailers, and Play / Resume, More information and
  Favorite buttons; stays pinned while you scroll
- Top 10 This Week rows for movies and shows, ranked from what people on the server watched
- Halloween, Christmas and Easter rows and falling effects that follow the calendar; a "<Library> Christmas" row
  for libraries that are mostly Christmas titles
- Because You Watched, genre, Collections, Watch Again and weekly Picks rows
- Theater Mode, a "Now Showing" sign-in screen, Jelly Cinema logo and icons, red accent color
- Phone, tablet, keyboard and TV-remote friendly
- English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch
- Every title is checked against the viewer's library access and rating limit; a built-in permission check
  confirms it for every account
- Open browsers reload once by themselves after an update
